📖 Rules & Regulations

Everything you need to know before playing

1

Entry Fee

Entry fee is Nu. 500 per player. Pay via online or cash and upload your payment screenshot. An admin will verify your payment before you can enter the game and start predicting.

2

Point System

⚠️
Important
You can earn a maximum of 5 base points per match. Even if multiple conditions apply, only the highest single tier is awarded — they do not stack.

💡
Bonuses vs. Badges
Bonuses add points to your score for in-the-moment plays (streaks, combos). Badges recognize milestones you collect over the tournament. You can earn both for the same play.
4

Penalty Shootouts (Knockout Stage)

In knockout matches, the winner is whoever advances to the next round — including when the match is decided by a penalty shootout.

1
Recorded scoreline is after extra time
The score at the end of extra time (or 90 minutes if no extra time was needed). Penalties do not change the recorded scoreline.
2
Only the shootout winner matters
Not the shootout score. Whether a team wins on pens 4–2, 5–4, or 7–6 makes no difference to your points. You don't predict the penalty score.
3
Draw predictions require a penalty winner pick
If you predict a draw, you must also pick who wins on penalties. The app will ask you when you enter equal scores. This becomes your "winner pick" if the match goes to pens.
4
Non-draw predictions have automatic winner pick
If you predict a winner (non-draw), your winner pick is automatic. No extra question — the team you picked to win is your winner pick.
5
Picking the advancing team always earns at least 2 points
Even if your score and goal difference were wrong, getting the advancing team right guarantees the Winner tier (2 points).
💡
Quick Example

Argentina vs France ends 1–1, Argentina wins 4–2 on penalties:

5
Predicted 1–1 + Argentina on pens → 5 pts (exact score)
3
Predicted 2–2 + Argentina on pens → 3 pts (right goal difference)
2
Predicted Argentina 2–1 → 2 pts (right winner)
0
Predicted France 2–1 → 0 pts (wrong winner)

7

Tiebreakers

When two players have the same total points, rank is decided by:

1
Most exact scorelines
More exact scoreline 5-point picks ranks higher
2
Most right-margin picks
Correct winner and correct goal difference — 3-point picks
3
Most right-winner picks
Correct winner only — 2-point picks
4
Earliest signup
If still tied, the player who joined the league first ranks higher

So precision beats luck: a player with more exact scorelines will always rank above a player who scraped the same points through bonuses.

These tiebreakers apply to all rankings, including the final standings used for prize distribution.

8

Private Leagues

Create or join private leagues with friends and colleagues. Each league has its own mini-leaderboard. You must be a paid player to create or join a private league. Share your league's invite code to invite others.

9

Prediction Deadlines

Predictions lock at kick-off time. You can edit your prediction anytime before the match starts. Once the match begins, your prediction is frozen.

10

Fair Play

One account per person. Admins reserve the right to remove duplicate or fraudulent accounts. All decisions are final.
