📖 Rules & Regulations

Everything you need to know before playing

1

Entry Fee

Entry fee is Nu. 500 per player. Pay via online or cash and upload your payment screenshot. An admin will verify your payment before you can enter the game and start predicting.

2

Point System

⚠️
Important
You can earn a maximum of 5 points per match. Even if multiple conditions apply, only the highest single tier is awarded — they do not stack.

Prize Distribution

Total pool is split among the top 3 finishers on the final leaderboard:

🥇 1st Place
50% of pool
🥈 2nd Place
30% of pool
🥉 3rd Place
20% of pool
4

Prediction Deadlines

Predictions lock at kick-off time. You can edit your prediction anytime before the match starts. Once the match begins, your prediction is frozen.

5

Fair Play

One account per person. Admins reserve the right to remove duplicate or fraudulent accounts. All decisions are final.
